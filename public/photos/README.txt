Drop real, licensed photographs here and the site uses them automatically
(the illustrated placeholders are the fallback, never shipped stock photos).

Expected filenames — JPG, landscape, ~1600px wide or better:

  hero.jpg      homepage hero (mountains / Gergeti-style shot works best)
  airport.jpg   "Airport transfers" service card
  cityroad.jpg  "City to city" service card
  tour.jpg      "Tours & trips" service card
  group.jpg     "Group transport" service card
  school.jpg    "School transport" service card
  about.jpg     About section (a clean vehicle on a mountain road)

After adding files: commit and run `npm run ship`.

Destination-specific photos (must show the ACTUAL place — no substitutes):
  tours/<tour-slug>.jpg    e.g. tours/kazbegi-gergeti-day-trip.jpg
  routes/<route-slug>.jpg  e.g. routes/tbilisi-airport-tbilisi.jpg
